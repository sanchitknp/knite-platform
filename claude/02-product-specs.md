## Claims that must not be used

Carried over from knite.in but unverified or risky on Amazon:

- "Trusted by 10,000+ households", "4.8 from 1,170+ reviews" — no such review base
- "2019 Year Founded" — the LLP was incorporated April 2026
- "3,262 cities", "14,870 service partners"
- Health claims: "supports immunity and digestion", pH figures
- Competitor brand names
- Specific TDS figures ("3000 ppm", "2000 ppm") in visible copy
- "ISI Certified*" with an unexplained asterisk
- "Up to 45% water savings" — copied from a competitor, no measurement behind it
