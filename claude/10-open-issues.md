Ordered by impact.

## 1. Reviews — the binding constraint

Review counts are in low single digits across the range, against competitors in the hundreds to tens of thousands.

**Enrol Core, Shield and Edge in Vine.** Up to 30 units per ASIN, costs inventory not
cash, reviews in 2–4 weeks, and the Vine badge carries more weight than an ordinary
review. Nothing else on this list moves the needle as much.

membrane throughput, on Flux's MRP, and on warranty length. Details in the confidential

figure under Legal Metrology, and Amazon's fair-pricing crawlers read external sites.
