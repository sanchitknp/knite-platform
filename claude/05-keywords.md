### Brand search is internal

Searches on the brand term are almost entirely internal (founder and family), not market
demand. Exclude brand-term rows from analysis and do not fund brand-defence ads on them.

## Keyword structure that works

Five slots per listing in `WATER_PURIFICATION_UNIT`, each a distinct layer so they do not
cannibalise:

1. **Offer terms** — unconditional warranty, no AMC, free pre-filter, paid installation
2. **Category heads** — the money terms for that model's lane
3. **Features** — LPS, auto-flush, in-tank UV, antiscalant, GPD, stage count
4. **Vernacular** — see below
5. **Capacity, use case, dual-use spelling variants**

`POTABLE_WATER_FILTER` gives only **one** keyword slot — a real disadvantage if you list
a purifier there by mistake.

## Vernacular

High value, near-zero competition. Romanised beats Devanagari for practical use.

```
pani paani jal shudh saaf peene ka pani tamba kshariya jal filter machine ghar ke liye
jol (Bengali) · thanni (Tamil) · neeru (Telugu/Kannada) · vellam (Malayalam)
```
