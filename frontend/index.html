Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Everything you wanted to know about Knite RO water purifiers, installation, and service.

Which Knite RO water purifier is best for my home?
The Knite Core (₹8,000) is our essential 7-stage RO + UV model — ideal for couples and small families on municipal water. The Knite Shield (₹9,000) adds UF and a mineraliser stage for tougher water and larger households. The Knite Prime (₹10,000) is our flagship 9-stage model with food-grade copper infusion and alkaline mineralisation for maximum wellness.
How does the free installation work?
Once you order from Amazon or Flipkart, a Knite-certified technician contacts you within 24 hours to schedule a visit. Installation typically takes 60–90 minutes and includes mounting, plumbing connections, TDS calibration, and a hands-on demo. There are zero installation charges anywhere in India.
What is the warranty on a Knite RO purifier?
Every Knite purifier comes with a 1-year comprehensive warranty covering electronics, motor, and pump. Filters are consumables and not under warranty but typically last 6–12 months. Extended warranty plans are available at checkout.
How often do the filters need to be replaced?
In normal usage, sediment and carbon filters last 6–8 months, while the RO membrane lasts 18–24 months depending on input water TDS. Your unit's smart filter-life indicator alerts you when a change is due. Replacements can be booked through your AMC or as on-demand service via our app.
Do you provide service across India?
Yes — Knite operates a service-partner network across 3,200+ cities in India through our certified technician partnerships. Same-day or next-day service is available in 80+ tier-1 and tier-2 cities. Check the coverage map to see availability in your area.
What is the difference between RO, UV, and UF purification?
RO (Reverse Osmosis) removes dissolved salts, heavy metals, and TDS — essential for borewell or hard water. UV (Ultraviolet) kills bacteria and viruses. UF (Ultra-Filtration) removes physical impurities and microbes without electricity. Knite purifiers combine all three (RO+UV+UF) for complete protection.
Is the copper-alkaline water from Knite safe to drink daily?
Absolutely. Knite's copper-alkaline cartridges add controlled, food-grade copper ions and slightly alkaline minerals (pH 8.5–9.0) — both shown to support immunity and digestion. The dosing follows BIS guidelines for safe daily consumption.
How can I book an AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract)?
AMC plans start at ₹1,500/year and cover all routine servicing, filter replacements (parts extra), and unlimited callouts. You can book directly via WhatsApp on +91 91960 68335 or through the contact form below.
Where can I buy Knite water purifiers?
Knite RO purifiers are available on Amazon.in and Flipkart, with Prime delivery, no-cost EMI options, and easy returns. Direct enquiries are also welcome via our contact form or WhatsApp.
What is the typical lifespan of a Knite RO water purifier?
With proper maintenance and timely filter changes, a Knite purifier lasts 8–10 years. The RO membrane and electronic components are built to enterprise-grade durability standards. Our AMC partners help maximize longevity through preventive servicing.
