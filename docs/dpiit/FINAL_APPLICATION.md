## 2. Innovation Write-Up (for portal + upload)

### 2.1 What is the problem the startup is solving?

India's ~₹1.2 lakh-crore home-appliance and smart-device aftermarket runs on an informal network of more than **1.8 million field technicians** who install, repair, and service everything from RO water purifiers and geysers to air-conditioners, CCTV kits, and solar inverters. The market is plagued by three structural failures:

**(a) Customer trust has collapsed.** Brands and marketplaces ship products but outsource the "last-metre" installation to third-party agencies. Customers face no-shows, inflated on-site quotes, unverified technicians, and no effective redressal — driving installation review scores below 3★ on every major e-commerce platform. This is particularly acute for RO water purifiers, where annual filter changes and periodic servicing are mandatory, and where poor service directly impacts household drinking-water safety.

**(b) Technician economics are broken.** A typical installer earns ₹8,000–₹20,000 per month, receives jobs through WhatsApp forwards from four or five competing aggregators, has no fixed schedule, waits 30–60 days for payment, and has zero structured upskilling path. In our primary field research across Kanpur and Lucknow (Feb–Apr 2026), 62% of technicians surveyed had never used a smartphone application beyond WhatsApp and Google Pay.

**(c) Brands fly blind.** OEMs and D2C sellers cannot observe installation SLAs, first-time-fix rates, or customer feedback at the job level. They cannot enforce quality, cannot attribute warranty failures to field causes, and cannot learn from patterns across pincodes or product lines.

Existing "technician apps" — Urban Company, NoBroker Services, Zimmber, and several B2B dashboard SaaS products — all require Android application downloads, Play Store accounts, continuous 4G data, and literacy with complex multi-screen interfaces. As a consequence, adoption among Tier-2 and Tier-3 technicians — where roughly 80% of installation volume sits — is negligible.

### 2.2 How does your startup propose to solve this problem?

Knite is a **WhatsApp-native field-service operating system combined with a first-party consumer hardware product**. Technicians never install an app. Every interaction — onboarding, KYC, job assignment, navigation, customer verification, and payout — happens inside a single WhatsApp thread with our business number, powered by **WhatsApp Cloud API Flows v5.1**.

A technician receives a job as a rich WhatsApp message with a tap-through flow: they see the full job card (address, customer contact, slot, payout), tap "Get Directions" (which sends a location pin and Google Maps deep-link into the same chat), tap "I Have Arrived" to start the SLA clock, and finally enter a 6-digit **Happiness Code** that the customer received at the time of booking. Entering the correct code closes the job, releases payment within 24 hours, and triggers a customer-satisfaction poll — also delivered on WhatsApp.

To prove the platform on a real product at scale, AXIOS ENTERPRISE LLP is launching its flagship consumer SKU, **Knite Pure+ (Mineral Edition)** — a 7-stage RO water purifier featuring a Powerflow 100 GPD pump, RO membrane, UV-C Pure Cycle, Ultrafiltration, Active Carbon Matrix, Minerals + Copper cartridge, and pH balancing. Every Knite Pure+ unit will be installed, AMC-serviced, and filter-replaced end-to-end through the WhatsApp-native Knite technician flow. This creates a closed-loop system: a real consumer product generating real technician jobs, building a certified RO-specialist network, and producing first-party revenue that funds the platform.

On the brand side, Knite will then open up as a multi-tenant SaaS platform: third-party RO manufacturers, OEMs, and service franchises will integrate through a REST API or via CSV upload, and receive a live dashboard with first-time-fix rate, mean time to arrive, technician-level NPS, and pincode-level heatmaps.

### 2.3 What is the uniqueness of your solution?

Four innovations make Knite structurally defensible:

**1. Zero-app technician onboarding.** Competing platforms require Android 9 or higher, a 200 MB install, Play Store sign-in, and continuous data. Knite onboards a technician with a single WhatsApp "Hi" — KYC upload (Aadhaar, PAN, bank proof), bank linking, training videos, and job dispatch all happen inside chat. This expands the addressable technician base by approximately four times relative to app-based competitors.

**2. Encrypted WhatsApp Flows with stateful data exchange.** Knite uses WhatsApp Flows v5.1 with RSA-2048 public-key cryptography and AES-128-GCM symmetric encryption for every data-exchange round-trip, combined with a custom Redis-backed session state machine that renders dynamic, multi-screen experiences (pending-jobs list → job detail → OTP verification → completion) without ever leaving the WhatsApp thread. We believe Knite is among the earliest Indian platforms to apply WhatsApp Flows to an end-to-end job lifecycle, not merely to lead capture.

**3. The Happiness Code — trust as a primitive.** A 6-digit code issued to the customer at booking, stored bcrypt-hashed, and verifiable only by the assigned technician at the customer's doorstep. This structurally prevents fraudulent "ghost closures", is tamper-evident in our audit log, and is observable by brand partners in real time. Being first validated on our own Knite Pure+ RO purifier, it becomes a proven primitive before being offered to third parties.

**4. Hardware-software flywheel.** Unlike pure marketplaces, Knite controls its own first consumer product (Knite Pure+). This guarantees a predictable stream of real installations, AMCs, and filter-change jobs to train the platform, certify technicians, and accumulate quality data — before onboarding external brands. No Indian competitor has this integration.

## 3. Business & Market Narrative

### 3.1 Why now

- **WhatsApp Cloud API + Flows (v5.1)** released in early 2026 — first version that supports encrypted, multi-screen, stateful workflows inside chat.
- **UPI AutoPay and Aadhaar-based e-KYC** now fully remote — enables instant onboarding of informal-sector workers.
- **India's bottled-water-to-RO transition** — RO water purifiers are becoming a near-universal household purchase, especially in Tier-2 cities where TDS levels are high and alternatives (bottled, packaged) are mistrusted. Ideal category to anchor Phase 1.
- **OEM margin compression** pushing brands to unbundle installation from sale.

### 3.2 Market sizing

| Horizon | Definition | Size |
|---|---|---|
| **TAM** | India aftermarket + installation services across all categories | ≈ ₹1.2 lakh crore / year |
| **SAM** | Home-services categories addressable via WhatsApp (RO, AC, geyser, CCTV, solar, etc.) in top 50 cities | ≈ ₹18,000 crore / year |
| **Immediate Serviceable Market (Yr-1–2)** | India RO water purifier installations + AMCs (new + existing base) | ≈ ₹9,000 crore / year |
| **SOM (Year 3)** | Knite Pure+ sales + platform GMV across 50 cities | ≈ ₹240 crore |

### 3.3 Go-to-market — three-phase plan

Knite's go-to-market is deliberately sequenced to build category depth before breadth. Instead of launching as a horizontal platform and chasing brand partnerships cold, we are first launching our own consumer product, using it to stress-test the WhatsApp-native operating system at real scale, and simultaneously building a proprietary, certified technician network in a single high-volume category — RO water purifiers.

- **Phase 1 (2026) — Launch Knite Pure+ (Mineral Edition); prove the platform on our own product.** Launch of our flagship 7-stage RO water purifier. Every unit sold is installed, AMC-serviced, and filter-replaced through the Knite WhatsApp flow. Delivers (i) a live, at-scale production deployment on a real consumer SKU; (ii) a certified RO-specialist technician database; and (iii) first-party revenue from sales + AMC funding the platform build-out.
- **Phase 2 (2027) — Open the platform to third-party RO brands.** With a proven workflow and a certified technician network live, we sign up established RO manufacturers and distributors as platform tenants for their installation, AMC, and service needs. Knite reduces their no-show rates, eliminates onboarding overhead, and gives them SLA + NPS dashboards — all without their building any of it.
- **Phase 3 (2028 and beyond) — Generalise across categories.** Extend horizontally to air-conditioners, geysers, CCTV, solar inverters, kitchen appliances, and smart-home devices. Same Flows, same Happiness Code, same brand dashboard. Knite becomes India's category-agnostic field-services operating system, in 22 languages.
